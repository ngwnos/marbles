# Jump chute

Reference: USD305443, top, perspective and end/side drawings.
https://patents.google.com/patent/USD305443S/en

Constant shared U section swept in the curve's normal frame. The receiving
post sits two 47 mm grid units above the lower support. Port spacing in plan is
140.1 mm, including the support's 24 mm lateral offset. Two rounded-end relief
slots interrupt the lower support's upper sleeve, as shown in the end view.
Dimensions beyond the shared fit remain reconstruction estimates.

Mesh is a single manifold with no collapsed/sliver triangles. Both ports pass
actual spacer intersections above and below. Five centered/off-center Box3D
launches and five full transfers through the landing outlet pass. The assembly
has a 16 mm open gap beyond the launch tip; its separate pieces are separate
connected collider meshes. Existing 70 offset-drop tests also pass.
