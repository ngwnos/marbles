# Finish lane

Source: USD305043, all six views.
https://patents.google.com/patent/USD305043S/en

A single recessed lane with a closed end, circular inlet head and wide rectangular
casing. The base sits on one plane; the lane and casing roof descend together.
The underside is cored, with the separate lower surface of the trough exposed.
Connector dimensions and lane width come from the common specification. Overall
length and broad casing dimensions are fitted proportions, not measurements.

Checks: one manifold, zero collapsed/sliver triangles, actual spacer fits above
and below the post, five drops settle at the end, and a six-marble queue remains
contained. Perspective and underside were inspected using the cached mesh in
Blender. Brave inspection remains pending while the Mac is locked.
