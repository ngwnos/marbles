# Extension tube

Reference: [isolated vintage extension, posts and adapter](https://www.ebay.com/itm/178284761370), especially photos 1–3. The long part is an uninterrupted pipe with no male collar; the separate adapter has a male end on both sides.

Tube diameter and socket diameter come from the shared connector spec. Length is fitted to six grid units (282 mm); the photo is not a calibrated measurement, so this count remains provisional. The ends have small edge breaks and accept the same 15 mm insertion from either direction.

One revolved hollow profile; no overlapping tube sections. Mesh audit, five offset drops and actual spacer insertion at each end pass. Offline perspective checked.
