# Starter funnel

Reference: official 2020–21 catalog page 14 and isolated underside photo in [the vintage accessory listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/278263486465).

Standalone circular bowl, rounded rim, smooth neck and female bottom socket. Uses the accepted funnel diameter and the shared drain-shell construction introduced for the starting gate. Reconstruction dimensions remain provisional.

One connected manifold with no collapsed or sliver triangles. Five off-center drops and the common spacer insertion pass. Offline perspective checked.
