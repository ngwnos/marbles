# Landing ramp

Source: USD305046, all seven orthographic/perspective views.
https://patents.google.com/patent/USD305046S/en

Semicircular tray with an open front and terminal C-shaped outlet. Curved backstop
has a horizontal rim; the planar floor falls toward the outlet. Reconstruction
uses a 55 mm arc radius and the common 47 mm shoulder / 62 mm tip. The patent does
not specify dimensions; tray proportions remain inferred.

Validation: single closed manifold, zero collapsed/sliver triangles, five Box3D
landing drops discharge through the outlet, complete spacer fits above and below.
Offline Blender inspection uses the actual cached mesh and vertex normals.
Brave review remains pending while the Mac is locked. Jump-to-landing assembly
validation follows with the jump chute.
