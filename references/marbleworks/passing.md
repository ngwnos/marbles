# Passing lane

Reference: [original design USD305044, six views](https://patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/7d/17/1e/6e1a6a32f3c012/USD305044.pdf).

Alternating broad bypasses with a long divider and two triangular islands. Shared port span, shoulder, bore, rolled rim and U-channel section. Dimensions follow the project reconstruction scale, not factory measurements.

Built as one footprint with holes, lofted through the shared wall section; all connector cores are subtracted after the mold is joined. No independent island caps or overlapping track meshes.

Validation: one connected manifold, no collapsed triangles or slivers, five offset drops discharge through the outlet. Top render checked against the design. Browser inspection remains pending while macOS is locked.
