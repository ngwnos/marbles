The three colored sections make two and a half turns. There are two external support bars, joint straps and rounded fastening heads. The inlet is a closed receiving cup with an upper male connector; the outlet has a female socket below, with no male connector above (the previous turn occupies that space). The full fall is two standard stacking units. Port spacing uses the common reconstruction span. Radius and pitch remain provisional fits to the photograph.

The assembled preview is one blue solid with a continuous U-section and cavity swept along the helix. The cavity height is bounded so it cannot cut through the turn above. The inlet bottom follows the receiving slope at constant floor thickness. Rods and tabs remain outside the running channel. All mold pieces are united before the cavity is subtracted.
